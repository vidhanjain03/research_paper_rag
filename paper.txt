TSRNet: A Lightweight Thermal Saliency Refinement Network for Tiny Human Detection
[Detecting humans in aerial thermal imagery is pivotal for search and rescue operations, particularly in environments with limited visibility. However, performance often suffers from atmospheric interference and complex background clutter that obscures tiny human targets. To address these degradations, we propose the lightweight Thermal Saliency Refinement Network (TSRNet) to retain weak thermal signatures. Our architecture introduces two novel modules, DFR and ACP. The Dual-Frequency Refinement (DFR) module leverages frequency domain analysis to suppress background noise while enhancing the saliency of target details. Complementing this, the Axial Context Pooling (ACP) module identifies salient regions by independently pooling features across spatial and channel dimensions to capture global context. Experiments on the RGBT Tinyperson benchmark demonstrate the superiority of our method, achieving 0.454 mAP@0.5 with a computational cost of only 36.5 GFLOPs.]

SAPNet: A Lightweight and Efficient Saliency-Aware Path Network for Tiny Human Detection in Thermal Aerial Imagery
[In aerospace applications, human detection from aerial thermal imagery has become increasingly important for surveillance and search-and-rescue missions. Designing a system that is accurate, efficient, and hardware-friendly remains challenging, as existing approaches often struggle with reliable detection and are computationally heavy. To address this, we propose a lightweight Saliency-Aware Path Network (SAPNet) that retains salient thermal signatures while leveraging dynamic path allocation to manage computational resources. The network introduces two novel modules: the Saliency-Aware Path and Channel-Reweighting (SPCR) module, which acts as a content-aware feature extractor to preserve subtle thermal signatures while reducing spatial and computational complexity, and the S3-CSP module, which enhances salient information aggregation from fused multi-scale features in the neck region. Extensive experiments on the TinyPerson benchmark demonstrate the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed method, achieving 0.404 mAP@0.5 with only 3.4M parameters and 20 GFLOPs, highlighting its suitability for resource-constrained aerospace platforms.]

Consensus Across Scale: Cross-Scale Correlation Guided Attention Network for Robust Tiny Human Detection in Aerial Thermal Imagery
[In defence and surveillance applications, human detection from aerial thermal imagery has become increasingly important. Designing a system that is accurate and addresses the tiny object problems remains challenging, as existing approaches often ignore the underlying problem of background noise and low Signal-To-Noise Ratio (SNR). To address this, we propose a Correlation-Guided Attention Network (CGANet) that maintains intra-level coherence and inter-level consistency. The network introduces the Correlation-Guided Adaptive Attention (CGAA) module, which incorporates the Cross-Scale Correlation (CGA) Module, sequentially followed by Balanced Semantic Fusion (BSF). The CGAA module establishes global consistency at each scale, combining it with multi-level correlation through CGA to keep features uniform across all scales, ultimately improving the SNR. The BSF fuse the low-level feature maps to enhance the tiny object representation. Experiments conducted on the TinyPerson benchmark confirm the success of our proposed method. By securing scores of 0.468 mAP@0.5 and 0.163 mAP@0.5-0.95, the results clearly demonstrate the model's advantage over existing competing techniques.]

C2SNet: Center-Surround Context-Aware Spatial \\ Network for Aerial Thermal Tiny Human Detection
[Human detection in aerial thermal imagery is a critical task for defence, surveillance, and aerospace applications, particularly during nighttime operations. However, developing an accurate method for robust detection of tiny objects remains a difficult challenge. Current techniques often struggle to retain critical spatial features and tend to overlook degradations specific to thermal imaging. Additionally, issues with feature misalignment and the complex fusion of local and global contexts further complicate the task. To address this, we propose the Center-Surround Context-Aware Spatial Network (C2SNet), an architecture that preserves salient spatial and semantic representations while ensuring precise feature alignment during fusion. The network introduces three novel modules. First, the Center-Surround Weighted-Channel (CSWC) module captures salient object features at different stages while suppressing background interference. Second, the Context-Aware Attention (CAA) module establishes global contextual awareness necessary to reliably differentiate between the object of interest and background clutter. Third, the Spatial-Semantic Fusion (SSF) module effectively combines spatial and semantic features, ensuring pixel-wise alignment between representations. Experiments conducted on TinyPerson, SIRST-V2 and HIT-UAV benchmark datasets confirm the effectiveness of our method. The results clearly demonstrate the model's efficacy over existing competing techniques.]
